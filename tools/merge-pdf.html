    How it works

    Each PDF you add is read and its pages are copied, in order, into one new PDF document — entirely inside your browser using an open-source PDF library. Files are never uploaded to a server.

    Example

    Add report-part1.pdf and report-part2.pdf, merge, and download a single combined PDF with all pages from both files in order.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Encrypted or password-protected PDFs generally cannot be read by the browser-based library used here. Remove the password first using a PDF editor, then merge.

    Is there a limit on how many files I can merge?

    There's no hard limit, but very large or numerous PDFs may be slow to process depending on your device's memory.
