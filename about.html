About ToolNagri

ToolNagri is a free online utility hub built for Indian students and professionals. We started with one idea: everyday calculations and document tasks — SGPA, EMI, attendance, PDF conversion — shouldn't require an app download, an account, or a fee.

What we believe

  • Tools should be free and fast, with no unnecessary signup.
  • Your documents and personal data are yours — we process what we can directly in your browser.
  • Good tools explain themselves. Every calculator on this site explains how the number was calculated.

We are a small, independent project. If something is broken or missing, let us know.
